Note # 1
1. The Commissioner, Urban Services, has submitted (Flag A) that a storm water drain at Ward No.21, Seetharampuram Municipal Corporation was breached in the heavy rains of 18-09-2026, and has proposed to accord administrative sanction for Rs.1.30 crore for emergency restoration.
2. As per Circular Memo No.USD01-WRKS0/9/2023, Dt.11-07-2023 (Flag B), the Commissioner may accord administrative sanction for emergency works up to Rs.1.50 crore per work. The proposed cost is within this limit.
3. It is therefore proposed that the Commissioner may be permitted to accord the sanction, and the same may be reported to the Government in the monthly statement.
