GOVERNMENT OF ANDHRA PRADESH
ABSTRACT
Urban Services – Works – Delegation of powers for according administrative sanction to works – Orders – Issued.
URBAN SERVICES (WORKS) DEPARTMENT
G.O.Ms.No.14 Dated: 18-03-2021
Read the following:
1. From the Commissioner, Urban Services, Lr.No.USD-W/412/2020, Dt.11-12-2020.
ORDER:
1. The Commissioner, Urban Services, in the reference read above, has requested the Government to delegate powers for according administrative sanction to works taken up by Urban Local Bodies, to avoid delays in execution.
2. Government, after careful examination, hereby delegate the following powers for according administrative sanction to works:
(i) Executive Engineer – up to Rs.10.00 lakhs per work.
(ii) Superintending Engineer – up to Rs.25.00 lakhs per work.
(iii) Chief Engineer – up to Rs.40.00 lakhs per work.
(iv) Commissioner, Urban Services – up to Rs.50.00 lakhs per work.
(v) Works costing above Rs.50.00 lakhs – Government.
3. Splitting of works to bring them within the delegated limits is strictly prohibited.
B. RAJASEKHAR
SECRETARY TO GOVERNMENT
