GOVERNMENT OF ANDHRA PRADESH
Circular Memo No.USD01-WRKS0/9/2023 Dated: 11-07-2023
Sub: Urban Services – Works – Monsoon 2023 – Emergency works – Powers of the Commissioner – Instructions – Reg.
Ref: G.O.Ms.No.14, Urban Services (Works) Department, Dt.18-03-2021.
1. In view of the heavy rainfall forecast during the monsoon season, Urban Local Bodies are required to take up emergency works such as restoration of drains and culverts without delay.
2. In order to avoid delays, it is clarified that the Commissioner, Urban Services, may accord administrative sanction for emergency works up to Rs.1.50 crore per work.
3. Such sanctions shall be reported to the Government every month.
B. RAJASEKHAR
SECRETARY TO GOVERNMENT
