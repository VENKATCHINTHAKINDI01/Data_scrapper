GOVERNMENT OF ANDHRA PRADESH
STANDING ORDER No.2/2020 Dated: 02-01-2020
Sub: Establishment – Processing of requests for transfer of staff – Procedure to be followed – Standing Order – Issued.
1. The following procedure shall be followed in the Department for processing requests for transfer of staff working in the Department and its regional offices.
(i) The dealing assistant shall verify and record in the note: (a) the station period of the employee; (b) the number of transfers already effected in the category against the ceiling prescribed in the transfer guidelines in force; (c) the Vigilance clearance status; and (d) the availability of a clear vacancy at the station requested.
(ii) The note shall cite the transfer guidelines currently in force by G.O. number and date.
(iii) The file shall be submitted through the channel: Section Officer – Assistant Secretary – Deputy Secretary – Secretary.
(iv) The Deputy Secretary may recommend, but orders shall be passed only by the Secretary to Government.
(v) Requests received outside the period of relaxation of the ban on transfers shall be kept pending for consideration during the next general transfers, unless the Government in the Finance Department has relaxed the ban.
2. This Standing Order shall be brought to the notice of all staff of Estt. sections.
B. RAJASEKHAR
SECRETARY TO GOVERNMENT
