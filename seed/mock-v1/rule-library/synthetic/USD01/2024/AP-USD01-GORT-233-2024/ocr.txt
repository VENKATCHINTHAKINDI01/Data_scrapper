GOVERNMENT OF ANDHRA PRADESH
ABSTRACT
Urban Services – Works – Seetharampuram Municipal Corporation – Construction of storm water drain in Ward No.14 – Administrative sanction for Rs.84.50 lakhs – Accorded.
URBAN SERVICES (WORKS) DEPARTMENT
G.O.Rt.No.233 Dated: 10-09-2024
Read the following:
1. G.O.Ms.No.27, Urban Services (Works) Department, Dt.06-08-2024.
2. From the Commissioner, Urban Services, Lr.No.USD-W/156/2024, Dt.22-08-2024.
ORDER:
1. In the reference 2nd read above, the Commissioner, Urban Services, has submitted proposals for according administrative sanction for construction of a storm water drain in Ward No.14 of Seetharampuram Municipal Corporation at an estimated cost of Rs.84.50 lakhs.
2. Government, after careful examination, hereby accord administrative sanction for Rs.84.50 lakhs (Rupees Eighty four lakhs and fifty thousand only) for the above work, as per the abstract estimate appended to this order.
3. The expenditure shall be met from the General Funds of the Municipal Corporation.
ANNEXURE – Abstract estimate
Earthwork excavation: Rs.4.20 lakhs
RCC drain (1.2m x 1.0m), 860 m: Rs.61.35 lakhs
Culvert crossings (4 nos.): Rs.9.80 lakhs
Shifting of utilities: Rs.3.15 lakhs
GST and contingencies: Rs.6.00 lakhs
Total: Rs.84.50 lakhs
R. VASANTHA KUMARI
SECRETARY TO GOVERNMENT
