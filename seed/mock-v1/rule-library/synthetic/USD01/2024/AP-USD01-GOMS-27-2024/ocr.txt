GOVERNMENT OF ANDHRA PRADESH
ABSTRACT
Urban Services – Works – Revision of delegation of powers for according administrative sanction to works, including emergency works – Orders – Issued.
URBAN SERVICES (WORKS) DEPARTMENT
G.O.Ms.No.27 Dated: 06-08-2024
Read the following:
1. G.O.Ms.No.14, Urban Services (Works) Department, Dt.18-03-2021.
2. From the Commissioner, Urban Services, Lr.No.USD-W/77/2024, Dt.19-04-2024.
ORDER:
1. In the G.O. 1st read above, Government delegated powers for according administrative sanction to works. In the reference 2nd read above, the Commissioner has requested revision of the limits in view of the increase in costs.
2. Government, after careful examination, and in supersession of the orders issued in the G.O. 1st read above, hereby delegate the following powers:
(i) Executive Engineer – up to Rs.15.00 lakhs per work.
(ii) Superintending Engineer – up to Rs.40.00 lakhs per work.
(iii) Chief Engineer – up to Rs.75.00 lakhs per work.
(iv) Commissioner, Urban Services – up to Rs.1.00 crore per work.
(v) Emergency works: the Commissioner may accord administrative sanction up to Rs.1.00 crore per work, subject to ratification by the Government within thirty (30) days.
(vi) Works (including emergency works) costing above Rs.1.00 crore – Government only. Works costing above Rs.5.00 crore shall require the concurrence of the Finance Department.
3. Splitting of works to bring them within the delegated limits is strictly prohibited.
R. VASANTHA KUMARI
SECRETARY TO GOVERNMENT
