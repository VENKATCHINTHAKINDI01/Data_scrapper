GOVERNMENT OF ANDHRA PRADESH
ABSTRACT
Public Services – Human Resources – General Transfers of Employees for the year 2019 – Guidelines – Orders – Issued.
FINANCE (HR.I-PLG. & POLICY) DEPARTMENT
G.O.Ms.No.62 Dated: 14-06-2019
Read the following:
1. G.O.Ms.No.21, Finance (HR.I) Department, Dt.03-05-2018.
2. From the Chief Secretary, U.O.Note No.CS-114/2019, Dt.07-06-2019.
ORDER:
1. The Government have been reviewing the policy on transfers of employees with a view to ensuring that employees are posted where their services are most needed, while giving due regard to their genuine requirements.
2. Accordingly, the Government hereby issue the following guidelines for general transfers of employees for the year 2019:
(i) The ban on transfers is relaxed for the period from 15-06-2019 to 30-06-2019.
(ii) Employees who have completed five (5) years of service at a station as on 31-05-2019 shall be transferred.
(iii) Employees who have completed less than two (2) years at a station shall not be transferred except on request or on administrative grounds.
(iv) Transfers in any category shall not exceed 20% of the cadre strength of that category.
(v) Preference may be given to spouse cases where both spouses are employees of the State Government, subject to availability of vacancy.
(vi) Employees retiring on or before 31-05-2021 shall not be disturbed except on request.
(vii) In respect of Section Officers and below in the Secretariat Departments, the competent authority to order transfers is the Secretary to Government of the Department concerned.
3. The ban on transfers shall come into force again with effect from 01-07-2019.
4. All Departments of Secretariat and Heads of Departments shall take necessary action accordingly.
D. KALYANI
PRINCIPAL SECRETARY TO GOVERNMENT
