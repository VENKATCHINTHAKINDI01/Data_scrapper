GOVERNMENT OF ANDHRA PRADESH
ABSTRACT
Public Services – Human Resources – General Transfers 2019 – Extension of the period of relaxation of ban on transfers – Amendment – Orders – Issued.
FINANCE (HR.I-PLG. & POLICY) DEPARTMENT
G.O.Ms.No.71 Dated: 28-06-2019
Read the following:
1. G.O.Ms.No.62, Finance (HR.I-Plg. & Policy) Department, Dt.14-06-2019.
ORDER:
1. In the G.O. read above, Government have issued guidelines for general transfers for the year 2019 and relaxed the ban on transfers from 15-06-2019 to 30-06-2019.
2. Several Departments have represented that, on account of the large number of requests received, the process could not be completed within the stipulated time.
3. Government, after careful examination, hereby issue the following amendment to the G.O. read above:
AMENDMENT
5. In para 2(i) of the G.O. read above, for the words and figures “30-06-2019”, the words and figures “10-07-2019” shall be substituted; and in para 3, for “01-07-2019”, read “11-07-2019”.
6. All other conditions stipulated in the G.O. read above shall remain unaltered.
D. KALYANI
PRINCIPAL SECRETARY TO GOVERNMENT
