GOVERNMENT OF ANDHRA PRADESH
Circular Memo No.FIN01-HROPDPP(OUTS)/7/2024 Dated: 27-03-2024
Sub: Public Services – Continuation of contract and outsourcing services in all Departments up to 31-03-2025 – Orders – Reg.
Ref: G.O.Ms.No.40, Finance (HR.I-Plg. & Policy) Department, Dt.28-04-2020.
1. In the reference cited, Government have ordered that no services shall be contracted or outsourced, or continued, without the prior concurrence of the Finance Department.
2. Government hereby permit continuation of the existing contract and outsourcing services in all Departments, which were in position as on 31-03-2024, for a further period up to 31-03-2025, on the same terms and conditions.
3. No fresh engagement shall be made under the cover of this Memo. Departments shall obtain the specific concurrence of the Finance Department for any new engagement.
M. HARINATH
SPECIAL SECRETARY TO GOVERNMENT
