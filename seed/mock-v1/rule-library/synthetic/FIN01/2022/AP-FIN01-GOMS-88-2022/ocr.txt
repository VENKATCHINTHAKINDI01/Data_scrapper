GOVERNMENT OF ANDHRA PRADESH
ABSTRACT
Public Services – Human Resources – General Transfers of Employees for the year 2022 – Guidelines – Orders – Issued.
FINANCE (HR.I-PLG. & POLICY) DEPARTMENT
G.O.Ms.No.88 Dated: 03-06-2022
Read the following:
1. G.O.Ms.No.62, Finance (HR.I-Plg. & Policy) Department, Dt.14-06-2019.
2. G.O.Ms.No.71, Finance (HR.I-Plg. & Policy) Department, Dt.28-06-2019.
3. G.O.Ms.No.19, General Administration (Ser.A) Department, Dt.09-02-2018.
ORDER:
1. The Government is committed to the welfare of its employees and to the efficient delivery of public services, and has decided to permit general transfers for the year 2022.
2. Accordingly, in supersession of the orders issued in the references 1st and 2nd read above, the Government hereby issue the following guidelines for general transfers of employees for the year 2022:
(i) The ban on transfers is relaxed for the period from 08-06-2022 to 30-06-2022.
(ii) Employees who have completed five (5) years of service at a station as on 31-05-2022 shall be transferred compulsorily.
(iii) Employees who have completed less than two (2) years at a station shall not be transferred except on request or on administrative grounds.
(iv) Transfers in any category shall not exceed 20% of the cadre strength of that category.
(v) Spouse cases, where both spouses are State Government employees, shall be given preference subject to availability of vacancy.
(vi) Employees retiring on or before 31-05-2024 shall not be disturbed except on request.
(vii) The competent authority for transfers of Section Officers and below in the Secretariat Departments shall be the Secretary to Government of the Department concerned; for Assistant Secretaries and above, the General Administration (Ser.A) Department, as per the reference 3rd read above.
3. After 30-06-2022 the ban on transfers shall come into force again, and no transfer shall be ordered without the prior approval of the Government in the Finance Department.
4. All Departments shall complete the process within the stipulated period and upload the transfer orders on the HRMS portal.
T. SURYA PRAKASH
PRINCIPAL SECRETARY TO GOVERNMENT
