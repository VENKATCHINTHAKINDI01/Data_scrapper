Public Services – Continuation of contract and outsourcing services in all Departments for the period from 01-04-2025 to 31-03-2026 – Orders – Issued.
FINANCE (HR.I-PLG. & POLICY) DEPARTMENT
G.O.Rt.No.1184 Dated: 21-03-2025
Read the following:
1. G.O.Ms.No.40, Finance (HR.I-Plg. & Policy) Department, Dt.28-04-2020.
2. Circular Memo No.FIN01-HROPDPP(OUTS)/7/2024, Finance (HR.I) Department, Dt.27-03-2024.
ORDER:
1. In the reference 2nd read above, Government permitted continuation of contract and outsourcing services up to 31-03-2025.
2. Government, after careful examination, hereby permit continuation of the contract and outsourcing services which were in position as on 31-03-2025 for the period from 01-04-2025 to 31-03-2026, subject to the conditions laid down in the reference 1st read above.
3. Any continuation beyond 31-03-2026 shall require fresh orders of the Government.
