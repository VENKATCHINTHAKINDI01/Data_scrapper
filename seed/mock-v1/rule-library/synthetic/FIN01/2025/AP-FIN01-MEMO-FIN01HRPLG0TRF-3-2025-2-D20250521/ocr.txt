GOVERNMENT OF ANDHRA PRADESH
Memo No.FIN01-HRPLG0(TRF)/3/2025-2 Dated: 21-05-2025
Sub: Public Services – General Transfers 2025 – Computation of station period – Clarification – Issued.
Ref: G.O.Ms.No.31, Finance (HR.I-Plg. & Policy) Department, Dt.12-05-2025.
1. In the reference cited, Government have issued guidelines for general transfers for the year 2025. Several Departments have sought clarification on the computation of the station period of five years.
2. In this regard, the following clarifications are issued:
(i) The station period shall be computed with reference to the total continuous service at a station, in any post or capacity, as on 30-04-2025.
(ii) Periods of leave not exceeding six months shall be counted towards the station period. Periods of deputation outside the Department shall not be counted.
(iii) Where an employee has been transferred within the same headquarters town, the service at both offices shall be treated as service at one station.
3. All Departments are requested to follow the above clarifications scrupulously.
M. HARINATH
