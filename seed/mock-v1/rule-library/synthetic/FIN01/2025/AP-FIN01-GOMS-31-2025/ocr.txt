ORDER:
1. The Government have decided to permit general transfers of employees for the year 2025, keeping in view administrative requirements and the need for transparency in postings.
2. Accordingly, in supersession of the orders issued in the reference 1st read above, the Government hereby issue the following guidelines for general transfers of employees for the year 2025:
(i) The ban on transfers is relaxed for the period from 15-05-2025 to 31-05-2025.
(ii) Employees who have completed five (5) years of service at a station as on 30-04-2025 shall be transferred compulsorily.
(iii) Employees who have completed less than two (2) years at a station shall not be transferred except on request or on administrative grounds.
(iv) Transfers in any category shall not exceed 15% of the cadre strength of that category. No relaxation of this ceiling shall be made without the prior concurrence of the Finance Department.
(v) Spouse cases, where both spouses are State Government employees, shall be given preference subject to availability of vacancy.
(vi) Employees retiring on or before 31-05-2027 shall not be disturbed except on request.
(vii) Requests for transfer of employees against whom disciplinary proceedings are pending or contemplated shall not be considered until the conclusion of such proceedings. Clearance of the Vigilance section is mandatory, as per the reference 3rd read above.
(viii) The competent authority for transfers of Section Officers and below in the Secretariat Departments shall be the Secretary to Government of the Department concerned; for Assistant Secretaries and above, the General Administration (Ser.A) Department.
3. After 31-05-2025 the ban on transfers shall come into force again. No transfer shall thereafter be ordered without the prior approval of the Government in the Finance Department.
4. All proposals shall be processed only through e-Office, and the HRMS ID of the employee shall be recorded in every transfer order.
T. SURYA PRAKASH
