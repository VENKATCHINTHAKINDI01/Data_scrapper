GOVERNMENT OF ANDHRA PRADESH
ABSTRACT
Public Services – Contract and Outsourcing services in Government Departments – Prior concurrence of Finance Department – Instructions – Orders – Issued.
FINANCE (HR.I-PLG. & POLICY) DEPARTMENT
G.O.Ms.No.40 Dated: 28-04-2020
Read the following:
1. G.O.Ms.No.151, Finance (HR.I) Department, Dt.14-07-2006.
ORDER:
1. It has come to the notice of the Government that certain Departments have engaged personnel on contract and outsourcing basis without obtaining the prior concurrence of the Finance Department, resulting in unauthorised commitments on the State exchequer.
2. Government hereby order that no services shall be contracted or outsourced, and no existing engagement shall be extended or continued, without the prior concurrence of the Finance Department and the approval of the Government.
3. Any engagement made in contravention of these orders shall be treated as irregular and the expenditure shall be recovered from the officer responsible.
4. These orders shall be in force until further orders.
T. SURYA PRAKASH
PRINCIPAL SECRETARY TO GOVERNMENT
