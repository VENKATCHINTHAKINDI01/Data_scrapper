GOVERNMENT OF ANDHRA PRADESH
ABSTRACT
Secretariat Service – Transfers and Postings of Officers in the Secretariat Departments – Competent authority – Orders – Issued.
GENERAL ADMINISTRATION (SERVICES-A) DEPARTMENT
G.O.Ms.No.19 Dated: 09-02-2018
Read the following:
1. G.O.Ms.No.304, General Administration (Ser.A) Department, Dt.17-09-2009.
ORDER:
1. Government have examined the question of competent authority for ordering transfers and postings of officers in the Secretariat Departments.
2. Government hereby order as follows:
(i) Transfers and postings of Assistant Secretaries and officers above that rank in the Secretariat Departments shall be ordered by the General Administration (Ser.A) Department only.
(ii) Transfers and postings of Section Officers, Assistant Section Officers and other staff within a Secretariat Department shall be ordered by the Secretary to Government of that Department.
(iii) No officer below the rank of Secretary to Government shall order transfers of Section Officers.
3. These orders shall come into force with immediate effect.
N. PADMAVATHI
SECRETARY TO GOVERNMENT (SER.)
