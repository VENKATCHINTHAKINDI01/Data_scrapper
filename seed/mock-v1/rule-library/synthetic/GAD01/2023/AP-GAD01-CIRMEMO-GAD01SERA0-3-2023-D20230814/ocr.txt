GOVERNMENT OF ANDHRA PRADESH
Circular Memo No.GAD01-SERA0/3/2023 Dated: 14-08-2023
Sub: Public Services – Transfers of employees facing vigilance enquiries / disciplinary proceedings – Prior clearance of Vigilance section – Instructions – Issued.
Ref: G.O.Ms.No.19, General Administration (Ser.A) Department, Dt.09-02-2018.
1. It has been brought to the notice of the Government that in some cases employees against whom vigilance enquiries or disciplinary proceedings were pending were transferred or relieved without the knowledge of the Vigilance wing, thereby hampering the enquiries.
2. All Departments are therefore instructed that no employee against whom a vigilance enquiry or disciplinary proceeding is pending or contemplated shall be transferred or relieved without the prior clearance of the Vigilance section of the Department.
3. Every note proposing the transfer of an employee shall specifically record the Vigilance clearance status.
4. These instructions shall be followed scrupulously.
N. PADMAVATHI
SECRETARY TO GOVERNMENT (SER.)
